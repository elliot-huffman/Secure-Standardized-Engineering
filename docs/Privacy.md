# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 17, 2026<br>
**Website:** https://sse.elliot.huffman.me<br>
**Owner/contact:** Elliot Huffman — elliot@huffman.me

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, why it is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.
This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages.
I use a small number of analytics and site-improvement tools to understand how people use the site, improve the content, troubleshoot issues, and make the site easier to use.

I do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## What this policy covers

This policy applies to your use of this website, including pages, articles, documentation, and other content available at: https://sse.elliot.huffman.me

This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from this site. If you follow a link to another website, that website's privacy policy applies.

## Information I collect

### Information you provide directly

In general, this website does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to read content.

If you contact me by email, form, social media, GitHub issue, or another method, I may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, username, message content, and any other details you include.

I use that information only to respond, maintain records, troubleshoot issues, or continue the conversation you started.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, some technical and usage information may be collected automatically by the hosting provider and analytics tools. This may include:

- Pages you visit
- Links or buttons you click
- Referring pages or search terms that brought you to the site
- Approximate location based on IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device type
- Operating system
- Screen size
- Date and time of visit
- Session activity, such as scrolling, navigation, and interaction patterns
- General performance and diagnostic information

This information helps me understand what content is useful, identify broken or confusing pages, improve site performance, and make the site easier to navigate.

## Analytics and tracking technologies

This website uses cookies, browser storage, scripts, and similar technologies to measure site usage and improve the visitor experience.

### Google Analytics

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect information about your visit, such as pages viewed, session duration, device and browser information, approximate location, and interactions with the site.

I use this information to understand which content is helpful, improve navigation, and maintain the site.

Google may process this information according to its own privacy practices. You can learn more here:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

You can also use Google’s browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics on supported browsers:

[Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### Google Tag Manager

I use Google Tag Manager to manage website tags and analytics scripts. Google Tag Manager is a tag management system. It helps load and organize tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.

Google Tag Manager itself is not used by me to identify you. However, the tags loaded through Google Tag Manager may collect information as described in this policy.

You can learn more about Google Tag Manager privacy practices here:

[Google Tag Manager data privacy and security](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/9323295)

### Microsoft Clarity

I use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site. Clarity provides tools such as behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. These help me see which parts of the site are useful, confusing, broken, or difficult to use.

Microsoft Clarity may collect information such as:

- Pages visited
- Clicks, scrolling, and navigation patterns
- Browser and device information
- Approximate location
- Session activity
- Referring pages
- Interaction and performance data

I use this information to improve site design, content quality, usability, and troubleshooting.

Microsoft Clarity is intended to mask sensitive information by default, such as text entered into form fields. I do not intentionally use Clarity to collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or sensitive personal information.

Microsoft may collect and use data according to the Microsoft Privacy Statement. You can learn more here:

[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement)

You can learn more about Microsoft Clarity here:

[Microsoft Clarity Privacy](https://clarity.microsoft.com/privacy)

## Cookies and consent

This website uses cookies, browser storage, scripts, and similar technologies for analytics and site improvement.

### Consent-based behavior

I use a consent banner to give you control over how your data is used.

- If you **accept cookies and tracking**, analytics tools such as Google Analytics and enhanced tracking features may be enabled.
- If you **decline**, I do not set non-essential cookies, and Google Analytics is not loaded.
- Some limited, privacy-respecting data collection may still occur where legally permitted without consent (see below).

Your choice is respected and can be changed at any time by clearing cookies or adjusting your browser settings.

---

### Analytics behavior by consent state

#### When you give consent

If you accept, I may use:

- Google Analytics for traffic and usage measurement
- Google Tag Manager to manage analytics scripts
- Microsoft Clarity with full feature set (such as behavioral insights)

These tools may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how the site is used and help improve it.

---

#### When you do NOT give consent

If you decline:

- **Google Analytics is not loaded**, and no Google Analytics cookies are set.
- **No non-essential cookies are used.**
- **Microsoft Clarity may still run in a reduced, privacy-focused mode**, limited to what is legally allowed without consent.

This reduced mode is designed to:
- Avoid collecting directly identifiable information
- Mask sensitive input fields
- Limit tracking to essential, aggregated, or anonymized behavioral data
- Support basic site improvement and troubleshooting

---

### Managing cookies

You can control cookies and tracking through:

- Browser settings (block or delete cookies)
- Private / incognito browsing
- Privacy-focused extensions
- The consent banner on this site
- The Google Analytics opt-out add-on:
  https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Blocking cookies may impact how some features behave.
