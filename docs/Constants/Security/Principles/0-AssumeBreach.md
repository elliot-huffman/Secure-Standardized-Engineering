# Assume Breach

The Principal of Assume Breach is a security mindset that assumes that an attacker has already gained access to your system.<br>
This means that you should design your systems with the assumption that they are already compromised, and take steps to minimize the damage that can be done.

The principal of assume breach can be used to derive most other security principles, such as least privilege, defense in depth, zero persistent admin, clean source and more!
